Rows show actual classes; columns show predicted classes. Diagonal cells are correct predictions. Other cells show classification mistakes.

What do these metrics mean?

Precision: among predictions for a class, how many were correct.

Recall: among actual images of a class, how many the model correctly identified.

F1: a combined measure of precision and recall.

Testing used a split from the same PlantVillage dataset. Exact pixel duplicates were excluded before splitting. Similar photos or different photos of the same leaf may still exist across splits. Performance on real-world phone photos has not yet been established.
